Native Audio & Multilingual Support

CSynchronized, high-quality audio in multiple languages and dialects. Bind unique voices to characters for consistent, professional audio-visual performance.

Multi-Shot Storyboarding

Generate up to 6 cuts in one go. Customize shot scales and angles with AI-driven transitions and dialogue patterns for a seamless cinematic rhythm

Industrial-Grade Subject Consistency

Lock character and object stability. Reference multi-angle images or videos to keep subjects lifelike across shots and camera movements.

15-Second Cinematic Output

Up to 15 seconds of continuous, 4K video. Perfect for complex sequences and plot development with photorealistic textures and precise text rendering.
